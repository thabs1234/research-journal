# Research: Website-Builder Options & Pricing in South Africa (2026)

## Sources
- corpus_wix.md — Self-serve builder options (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress.org, Google Sites) with ZAR pricing.
- corpus_sa_agencies.md — South African agency / done-for-you pricing and the SiteCraft SA model.

## Raw findings

### Wix (cloud drag-and-drop builder)
- Free plan includes Wix branding and a subdomain (sitename.wixsite.com).
- Paid plans in South Africa (2026, ZAR/mo, ex VAT), converted from USD list (~$17/$29/$49/$159 at ~18.5 ZAR/USD):
  - Light ~R105/mo
  - Core ~R180/mo
  - Business ~R310/mo
  - Business Elite ~R590/mo
- Pros: very easy, good templates, built-in booking and store. Cons: hard to migrate off, weaker SEO than WordPress.

### Squarespace (template-driven builder)
- No free tier; 14-day trial only. Stronger design than Wix.
- Plans (USD/mo, ~18.5 ZAR):
  - Personal ~$16 (R296)
  - Business ~$23 (R425)
  - Commerce Basic ~$28 (R518)
  - Commerce Advanced ~$52 (R962)
- Pros: polished design, good for portfolios/creative. Cons: less flexible, pricier.

### WordPress.org (self-hosted, free software)
- Software is free; you pay hosting only.
- Typical SA shared hosting ~R50–R150/mo (examples: Afrihost, Xneelo, HostAfrica).
- Pros: full control, best SEO, huge plugin ecosystem. Cons: you maintain it (updates, security).
- Usually paired with a page builder (e.g. Elementor) or a theme.

### Google Sites (free)
- Free with a Google account. No custom domain on free tier (sites.google.com/view/...). Extremely simple.
- Pros: zero cost, integrates with Workspace. Cons: limited design, not for serious business.

### South African agency / done-for-you pricing (2026)
- Basic 1–5 page brochure site: R1,500–R6,000 once-off, plus R300–R800/mo maintenance/hosting.
- Mid brochure site: R6,000–R15,000.
- E-commerce site: R12,000–R40,000+.
- Monthly retainer models: R1,500 setup + R450/mo (SiteCraft SA model) up to R2,500+ setup + R750–R1,500/mo for managed clients.
- Freelancers on Upwork/Fiverr: R500–R3,000 for a basic site, variable quality.

### SiteCraft SA (user's business)
- Model: R1,500 once-off setup + R450/month maintenance for a done-for-you local-business website.
- Positioning: undercuts agencies by ~4–10x on setup, recurring revenue model.
