South African Research

Research mapped onto South Africa's actual disease burden and market — the localised counterpart to the Deep Research stream. The flagship output is a mechanism-first health-communication content system a freelancer can sell to the DoH, provinces, and NGOs.

The gap this fills: 2025 PEPFAR cuts removed ~22% of South Africa's US$2.56B HIV budget — hitting community-based testing, psychosocial and education services. NGOs need cheap, scalable, local health-ed content to replace defunded community education. This stream is the research behind that product.

1 · Freelance brief — "Mechanism-First" SA health comms

source: msf_research/SA_healthcomms_freelance_pitch.md

We reverse-engineered a proven global health-TikTok format (@medicalsciencefacts, 32 real videos analysed — hernia 574K, breast-cancer 213K) and matched it to SA's real burden. The transferable lesson:

People engage with the HIDDEN MECHANISM of a real condition — not the outline of a thing they vaguely know.

SA's real burden (sourced: Wikipedia Health in SA / HIV in SA, 2025)

  • HIV: ~7.8M PLHIV — highest national burden on earth; ~50,000 deaths/yr; world's largest ART programme; U=U is real.
  • TB: run together with HIV by the DoH ("National HIV and AIDS and TB Programme"); co-infection is the killer.
  • NCDs rising: obesity climbing as hunger falls; hypertension, diabetes, stroke, cancer all growing.
  • Maternal/child: maternal mortality 410/100k; under-5 mortality 65/1k.

The mismatch we exploit

Global health-TikTok wins on hernia/cancer trivia; SA's lethal burden is HIV/TB/NCDs/maternal. Nobody runs the "what really happens when TB takes hold in your lungs?" format at SA scale, in SA languages, with SA clinic CTAs. That is the gap.

Ready-to-send pitch (one paragraph)

"South Africans spend hours a day on TikTok/WhatsApp but health departments still push PDFs. I produce mechanism-first short video — 'What really happens when TB takes hold in your lungs?' — that a verified global account proved drives 100K–570K engagement per clip. I localise it to SA's real burden (HIV/TB/NCDs/maternal), in English + Afrikaans + isiZulu, each clip ending on a real clinic action. It is cheap to produce, owned by you, and fills the community-education gap left by 2025 funding cuts. Pilot: 30 clips, one condition area, with engagement + recall metrics reported back."

Why a freelancer wins (vs an agency)

  • Cost: a 30-clip pilot at agency rates is R100k+; templated format delivers the same at a fraction.
  • Speed: templated scripts + localisation = days, not months of strategy decks.
  • Native feel: SA freelancer > overseas agency for language + clinic realism.
  • Funded buyers exist: NGOs with PEPFAR-shaped holes, CANSA, Heart & Stroke Foundation, Diabetes SA, provincial health comms.

4 · 30-Day SA Health-Education Content Calendar

source: msf_research/SA_health_content_calendar.md Built from the @medicalsciencefacts format. 1 clip/day, 20–45s vertical, calm documentary voice, curiosity-gap hook, "Follow for more" CTA, pinned "not medical advice" on condition videos. SA topics layered on their proven pillars (body mechanics → cancer/women's health → organs/SA public health → skin/sun/myth-busting). Posting cadence: 18:00–20:00 SAST weekdays; batch-record weekly.

HPCSA advertising rules apply to health claims — keep to education, no prescribing. Language A/B: English → Afrikaans (Volg vir meer!) → isiZulu captions for KZN/Gauteng reach.
