Why publish at all? The 2025 PEPFAR cuts removed ~22% of South Africa's HIV education budget. The SA stream turns open research into a cheap, localisable health-education content system a freelancer can deliver to provinces and NGOs — filling the gap the cuts left. That is the concrete output this journal exists to support.
