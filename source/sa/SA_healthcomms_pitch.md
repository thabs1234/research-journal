# Freelance Brief — "Mechanism-First" Health Comms for the SA Health Department & NGOs

**Prepared by:** Thabang (freelance health-content / creative-education)
**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Premise:** We reverse-engineered a *proven* global health-education TikTok format
(`@medicalsciencefacts`, 32 real videos analysed) and matched it to South Africa's
*actual* disease burden (sourced: Wikipedia Health in SA / HIV in SA, 2025 data;
SAMRC/WHO context). The output is a pitch + content system a freelancer can sell
to the DoH, provincial health, or NGOs (Right to Care, TAC, NICD, CANSA, heart/diabetes
foundations).

---

## 1. What the profile PROVES (the transferable lesson)

Not "post health facts." The lesson is sharper:

> **People engage with the HIDDEN MECHANISM of a real condition — not the outline
> of a thing they vaguely know.**

Evidence from 32 real videos (like counts captured live):
- 🔥 Winners = "watch it break/work": hernia forming (574K), breast-cancer
  diagnosis step-by-step (213K), leukemia in the marrow (37K), migraine in the brain
  (31K).
- 💤 Losers = "stages of something familiar": pregnancy (1.3K), milk (1.3K), obesity (860).
- Keystone hook: **"What really happens when…?" / "A look inside…"** — the body as a
  place you travel through.
- Repeatable atom: `name the thing → show the steps/mechanism → flag the danger →
  soft CTA`. No personality, no trends, no news. A lesson machine.

**This is the rare format that is portable across languages, cultures, and health
systems** — exactly what a national health-comms programme needs.

## 2. South Africa's REAL burden (the topic map to localise)

Sourced facts (Wikipedia Health in SA; HIV/AIDS in SA; 2024–2025):
- **HIV:** ~7.8M people living with HIV — *highest national burden on earth*.
  ~50,000 HIV deaths/year. World's largest treatment programme. **U=U is real**
  (undetectable = untransmittable).
- **TB:** Programmed *together* with HIV by the DoH ("National HIV and AIDS and TB
  Programme") — co-infection is the killer.
- **Maternal/child:** maternal mortality 410/100k; under-5 mortality 65/1k.
- **NCDs rising:** obesity climbing as hunger falls; hypertension, diabetes,
  stroke, cancer all growing.
- **Funding shock:** 2025 PEPFAR cuts hit community-based HIV testing, psychosocial
  and *education* services (22% of the $2.56B HIV budget). **That gap is a freelance
  opportunity** — NGOs need cheap, scalable, local health-ed content to replace
  defunded community education.

**The mismatch we exploit:** global health-TikTok wins on hernia/cancer trivia;
SA's lethal burden is HIV/TB/NCDs/maternal. Nobody is running the "what really
happens when TB takes hold in your lungs?" format at SA scale, in SA languages,
with SA clinic CTAs. That is the gap we fill.

## 4. The product (what you actually deliver)

A **content system**, not one-off posts:
1. **30-script pack per condition area** (already built for general health;
   re-skinnable to HIV/TB/NCDs/maternal in days).
2. **Localised variants** — English 🟢 ship / Afrikaans 🟢 / isiZulu 🟡 (native
   review) hooks (template exists: `lang_variants_top10.md`).
3. **Every clip carries a real SA CTA** — "Free HIV test at any clinic" / "TB test
   if cough >2 weeks" / "10177 for stroke" — turning views into clinic visits.
4. **Compliance baked in** — pinned "not medical advice"; no product pushes;
   aligns with DoH messaging (U=U, test-and-treat).
5. **Metrics report** — views, saves, shares, comment sentiment, CTA clicks
   (Gecho Bridge or native analytics).
