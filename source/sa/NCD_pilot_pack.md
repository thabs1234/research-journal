# NCD Pilot Pack — 10 mechanism-first scripts (SA-localised)

Companion to `HIV_TB_pilot_pack.md`. NCDs are SA's fast-rising burden: obesity
climbing as hunger falls, hypertension extremely common, diabetes accelerating,
stroke a leading cause of death/disability. Format proven by
`@medicalsciencefacts` (hernia 574K, breast-cancer diagnosis 213K — "what really
happens / look inside" hooks win). Each clip = 20–45s vertical, calm documentary
voice, ends on a REAL SA clinic/behaviour CTA. Compliance: pinned "not medical
advice"; aligns to DoH prevention messaging.

### 1 — Diabetes vessel damage  🔥 FLAGSHIP
**Title:** How diabetes silently damages your blood vessels — vessel by vessel. 🩸
**Hook:** How diabetes silently damages your blood vessels — vessel by vessel.
**Narration:** In type 2 diabetes, the body stops responding well to insulin, so
sugar builds up in the blood. Over years, that excess sugar quietly damages blood
vessels — in the eyes, kidneys, nerves, and heart. That's why uncontrolled
diabetes can lead to blindness, kidney failure, and amputations. The early signs —
thirst, frequent urination, fatigue — are easy to miss. In South Africa it's rising
fast. The good news: diet, movement, and medicine keep it under control. A free
blood-sugar test at your clinic shows where you stand.
**On-screen:** "Insulin resistance → high sugar → vessel damage | Eyes/kidneys/heart"

### 2 — Stroke BE FAST
**Title:** What really happens during a stroke — and how to spot it in 60 seconds. ⏱️
**Hook:** What really happens during a stroke — and how to spot it in 60 seconds.
**Narration:** A stroke is the brain's blood supply cut off. Brain cells start
dying within minutes. Use BE FAST: Balance — sudden loss? Eyes — blurred or double
vision? Face — does one side droop? Arms — can they raise both? Speech — slurred
or strange? Time — call emergency services immediately. In South Africa, stroke is
a leading cause of death and disability. Fast treatment saves brain and life. See
even one sign — don't wait, act now.
**On-screen:** "BE FAST: Balance, Eyes, Face, Arms, Speech, Time → 10177"
**Tags:** #StrokeAwareness #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Any sign of stroke — call 10177 (or 112) now.

### 3 — Hypertension silent strain
**Title:** High blood pressure rarely shows symptoms — but it's silently damaging you. 💔
**Hook:** High blood pressure rarely shows symptoms — but it's silently at work.
**Narration:** Blood pressure is the force of blood against your artery walls. Too
high for too long, and it silently strains the heart, kidneys, and brain. The
danger? Usually no warning signs — no pain — until a stroke or heart attack hits.
In South Africa it's extremely common, often linked to salt, stress, and weight.
The fix is real: less salt, more movement, medicine when needed. A quick free check
at any clinic tells you where you stand.
**On-screen:** "Silent strain on heart/kidneys/brain | Free check at clinic"
**Tags:** #Hypertension #HeartHealth #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Get your blood pressure checked free at a local clinic.

### 4 — How a heart attack happens
**Title:** What really happens during a heart attack — the blocked-artery chain. ❤️
**Hook:** What really happens during a heart attack — the blocked-artery chain.
**Narration:** Over years, fatty plaques build up inside coronary arteries — the
pipes feeding your heart muscle. One can crack, and a clot forms on top, sealing
the pipe shut. The heart muscle beyond it starts to die. That's a heart attack:
chest tightness, pain down the arm, sweat, breathlessness. In South Africa it's a
top killer. Know the signs, and call 10177 fast — every minute of muscle saved
matters.
**On-screen:** "Plaque → clot → pipe sealed → muscle dies | Call 10177"
**Tags:** #HeartAttack #HeartHealth #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Chest pain + arm pain + sweat — call 10177 now.

### 5 — Kidney filtration failure
**Title:** How kidneys fail quietly — and why you don't notice until it's late. 🩺
**Hook:** How kidneys fail quietly — and why you don't notice until it's late.
**Narration:** Healthy kidneys filter waste and extra fluid from your blood about
24 times a day. When they fail — from diabetes, high blood pressure, or infection —
waste builds up, ankles swell, you tire easily. The cruel part: kidneys fail
silently, with few symptoms until most function is gone. In South Africa both
dialysis and transplant are available through public hospitals. Watch for swelling
and low urine output — and control your sugar and pressure to protect them.
**On-screen:** "Kidneys filter 24x/day | Fail silently → swelling, fatigue"
**Tags:** #KidneyHealth #Diabetes #Hypertension #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Swelling or low urine output — see a clinician.

### 6 — How a clot forms (and why it's danger)
**Title:** What really happens when a blood clot forms — a look inside. 🩸
**Hook:** What really happens when a blood clot forms — a look inside.
**Narration:** When a vessel is damaged, tiny platelets rush to the spot and stick
together, weaving a mesh of protein to seal the leak — that's a clot, and it saves
your life from bleeding out. But sometimes a clot forms where it shouldn't, or
breaks free and travels — to the lung (a pulmonary embolism) or the brain (a
stroke). That's when helpful becomes hazardous. Moving after surgery or long
sitting, and treating clots early, keeps them on your side.
**On-screen:** "Platelets + mesh → seal leak | Wrong place = stroke/PE"
**Tags:** #BloodClot #Stroke #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Sudden leg swelling + breathlessness — seek care.

### 7 — Salt and blood pressure
**Title:** How too much salt quietly raises your blood pressure. 🧂
**Hook:** How too much salt quietly raises your blood pressure.
**Narration:** Salt pulls water into your blood. More water means more volume, and
more volume means higher pressure against your artery walls. Do it daily, for
years, and those walls take the strain — raising stroke and heart-attack risk.
South Africans often eat far more salt than they realise, hidden in bread,
processed meat, and stock cubes. Cutting back — tasting food before adding salt,
choosing fresh over processed — is one of the cheapest, strongest things you can do
for your heart.
**On-screen:** "Salt → water retained → higher pressure → strain"
**Tags:** #Salt #Hypertension #HeartHealth #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Lower salt; get a free blood-pressure check.

### 8 — Insulin resistance (pre-diabetes)
**Title:** What really happens in insulin resistance — before diabetes begins. 🔬
**Hook:** What really happens in insulin resistance — before diabetes begins.
**Narration:** Insulin is the key that lets sugar into your cells. In insulin
resistance, the lock gets stiff — cells ignore the key, so sugar stays in the
blood, and the pancreas pumps out more insulin to force the door. For years this
simmer before type 2 diabetes is diagnosed. The drivers: excess weight, inactivity,
family history. The reversal is real: movement, weight loss, and better food can
restore the lock. Caught early, diabetes is often preventable.
**On-screen:** "Cells ignore insulin → sugar stays high → pancreas overworks"
**Tags:** #InsulinResistance #Prediabetes #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Risk factors? Ask your clinic for a screening.

### 9 — Amputation chain (diabetic foot)
**Title:** How a small foot wound becomes a big danger in diabetes. 🦶⚠️
**Hook:** How a small foot wound becomes a big danger in diabetes.
**Narration:** In diabetes, nerve damage can numb the feet, so a cut goes unfelt.
Poor circulation and weak immunity mean it heals slowly — and high sugar feeds
infection. What starts as a blister can deepen into an ulcer, and threatens the
whole foot. That's the chain behind diabetic amputations. The break is simple:
check your feet daily, wear proper shoes, and control your sugar. One small habit
prevents life-changing loss.
**On-screen:** "Numb + poor circulation → ulcer → amputation risk"
**Tags:** #DiabeticFoot #Diabetes #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Foot wound not healing? See a clinician promptly.

### 10 — The cholesterol plaque build-up
**Title:** What really happens as cholesterol clogs your arteries. 🫀
**Hook:** What really happens as cholesterol clogs your arteries.
**Narration:** Low-density cholesterol slips into the artery wall, where it
triggers inflammation and slowly builds a fatty plaque. The pipe narrows. At first
nothing — then chest pain on effort, or a clot on the plaque that triggers a heart
attack. In South Africa, diet, weight, and genetics all play a role. The good news:
exercise, better food, and medicine can slow or even shrink plaques. Know your
numbers — a clinic lipid test shows your risk.
**On-screen:** "LDL → artery wall → plaque → narrowing"
**Tags:** #Cholesterol #HeartHealth #SouthAfrica #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts #foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts
**Pinned note:** Not medical advice. Ask your clinic about a cholesterol/lipid test.
