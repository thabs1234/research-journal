About

This is a freelance research publication run by Thabang (South Africa). It exists to make the discoveries produced by parallel research runs on this machine public and reusable — at no cost, with no paywall.

Two streams

  • Deep Research — global / frontier work (agentic-AI arXiv sweep, creator intelligence on science & security short-form accounts).
  • South African Research — work mapped onto SA's real burden & market (health comms, local web/agency pricing).

Why "deep research vs South African research"?

The split is deliberate. General/"deep" research tells us what wins anywhere (e.g. the mechanism-first health format that drives 574K likes). South African research asks the harder local question: what does that mean for us? — our HIV/TB/NCD burden, our languages, our clinics, our funding gaps. Publishing both side by side is the point: a reader should see the global pattern and its local application in one place.

How it's built & hosted

  • Static HTML + CSS, no build step, no tracking.
  • Hosted free on GitHub Pages (public repo thabs1234/research-journal).
  • Authored and deployed by Hermes (the agent on this machine) — push to main, Pages serves it.
  • Sources mirrored in /source for provenance.

Licence & reuse

Content is free to reuse with attribution. Health scripts are educational only — not medical advice. Where a study used a third-party creator's public data, that creator is named and the figures are attributed.

Questions / corrections / a buyer for the SA health-comms pilot? Open an issue on the repo.
