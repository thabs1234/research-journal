## Filming spec (low-budget, phone-OK)
- **Camera:** phone, 1080p/4K vertical, tripod or stable surface. Natural light
  (window) or ring light.
- **Audio:** phone mic OK for VO if quiet; lapel/buds mic better. Calm, slow,
  documentary tone — NOT shouting.
- **B-roll:** your own cough clip OR free stock (Pexels/Coverr, CC0). Animations:
  CapCut / Canva free, 1 palette (greens + neutral).
- **Edit:** CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (free). Captions burned-in (accessibility +
  sound-off viewing). 9:16 safe zones (text centered, 80% width).
- **Length:** 45s is ideal for TikTok; if you want the "2-minute sample" exactly,
  chain this + the Diabetes flagship + the Stroke BE FAST clip (from NCD pack) with
  a 3s transition each → ~2:10 reel.

## Compliance (non-negotiable for DoH/NGO pitch)
- Pinned comment: **"Not medical advice. Persistent cough >2 weeks — visit a clinic
  for a free TB test."**
- No product, no brand, no treatment claims beyond "curable with medicine / free
  test at clinic" (DoH-aligned).
- Keep language simple; offer AF/ZU dubbed version for the real campaign.
