### 1 — TB in the lungs  🔥 FLAGSHIP
**Title:** What really happens when TB takes hold in your lungs? 🫁
**Hook:** What really happens when TB takes hold in your lungs?
**Narration:** Tuberculosis is a bacterial infection. When someone with TB coughs,
they release tiny droplets — you breathe them in, and the bacteria settle in your
lungs. Your immune system often walls them off — that's latent TB, no symptoms. But
if it breaks free, it multiplies and causes a cough, fever, night sweats, and weight
loss. South Africa has one of the highest TB rates in the world. The good news: it's
curable with a course of medicine. A persistent cough? Get tested — free, at any clinic.
**On-screen:** "Cough droplets → lungs | Latent vs active | Curable with meds"

**Hook:** How HIV actually attacks your immune system — a look inside.

### 6 — How ARVs work (U=U mechanism)
**Title:** How antiretrovals work — and why "undetectable = untransmittable." 🛡️
**Hook:** How antiretrovals work — and why undetectable equals untransmittable.
**Narration:** Antiretrovals don't kill HIV completely — they block it from copying.
Taken daily, they drive the virus so low it can't be measured in blood — undetectable.
At that level, the virus can't damage your immune system, and it can't pass to a
sexual partner. That's U=U. In South Africa, ARVs are free and for life. The deal is
simple: test, start treatment early, take it every day — and live a long, healthy,
non-transmitting life.
**On-screen:** "ARVs block copying → undetectable → can't transmit (U=U)"

### 8 — The latent TB trap
**Title:** The latent TB trap — why silent infection can wake up. 😴
**Hook:** The latent TB trap — why silent infection can wake up.
**Narration:** Most people who breathe in TB bacteria never get sick. Their immune
system locks the bacteria in a dormant cage — latent TB, no symptoms, not contagious.
But if immunity drops — through HIV, malnutrition, or illness — the cage can break,
and active TB erupts. That's why people living with HIV are screened and often given
preventive therapy. Finding latent TB before it wakes is one of South Africa's
quietest, most effective saves.
**On-screen:** "Dormant TB → caged by immunity | Weak immunity → wakes up"

### 9 — Reading the CD4 count
**Title:** How a CD4 count tells the story of your immune system. 🔢
**Hook:** How a CD4 count tells the story of your immune system.
**Narration:** CD4 cells are your immune system's frontline soldiers. A healthy
adult has roughly 500 to 1,500 per drop of blood. HIV quietly picks them off — the
count falls, and defence weakens. That's why clinics check CD4 (or viral load) to
decide when to start or step up treatment. With ARVs, the count climbs back, soldiers
regroup, and the body recovers. One number, a whole picture of your defence.
**On-screen:** "Healthy: 500–1500 CD4 | HIV lowers it | ARVs rebuild it"
