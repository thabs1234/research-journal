# Top-10 Language Variants — Afrikaans + isiZulu Hooks
For the highest-burden SA disease/awareness clips (days 8,13,14,18,19,20,21,23,25,27).
Each gives the English hook (reference) + an AF and ZU opening hook to film a
second cut. Full scripts are in SA_health_scripts_30.md — translate the body the
same way.

**Confidence flags (read before posting):**
- 🟢 Afrikaans — high confidence (Germanic, close to English/Dutch).
- 🟡 isiZulu — MODERATE. Noun-class prefixes (is-/izi-/um-/aba-/im-) are easy to
  get wrong. **Have a native Zulu speaker review before publishing.** Medical
  accuracy matters; a wrong prefix won't break comprehension but looks unpolished.

## How to use
1. Film the English master (scripts file).
2. Re-record the hook in AF + ZU for these 10, keep the same B-roll.
3. Post all three under one topic, or A/B test which language wins reach in
   Gauteng (AF) vs KZN (ZU).
4. Keep the English pinned "not medical advice" note on every variant.
5. **Zulu: native review before publish.** Afrikaans is safe to ship.
