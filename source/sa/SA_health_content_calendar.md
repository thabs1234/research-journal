# 30-Day Health-Education TikTok Calendar (SA brand)
Built from the @medicalsciencefacts format. Calm documentary voice, curiosity-gap
hooks, awareness angle, "Follow for more" CTA. SA-relevant topics layered on top
of their proven body-science pillars.

## Format spec (copy their winning structure)
- **Length:** 20–45s vertical. One mechanism/topic per video.
- **Voice:** calm, educational, "ScienceExplained" — no hype, no dancing.
- **Hook (first 3s):** a question or a "what happens when…" gap.
- **Body:** "a journey through the body" / "from X to Y" narration.
- **CTA:** "Follow for more amazing body facts! 🔬" (or SA variant: "Volg vir meer!")
- **Always-on hashtags:** `#HumanBody #ScienceExplained #HealthEducation #MedicalFacts #BodyFacts #ScienceFacts` + topic tags + reach tags (`#foryou #facts #science #viral #shorts`).
- **Disclaimer (pinned on sensitive topics):** "Educational only — not medical advice. Speak to a clinician/Nurse at your local clinic."

## Hook templates (reuse)
1. `What really happens when you [action]? A journey through the human body.`
2. `Understanding [condition] — how it develops and why early action matters.`
3. `Your [organ] does something incredible every [timeunit]… but most people don't know it.`

## SA localization notes
- **Language variants:** pilot English; A/B test Afrikaans (`Volg vir meer!`) and
  isiZulu captions for reach in KZN/Gauteng. Keep medical terms accurate.
- **Clinic pointer:** every awareness video should name the free resource —
  "Visit your nearest clinic / call the TB/HIV helpline" — turns views into action.
- **Compliance:** pin "Not medical advice" on all condition videos; avoid
  prescribing. SA HPCSA advertising rules apply to health claims — keep to education.
- **Posting cadence:** 1/day at 18:00–20:00 SAST (after-work scroll); batch-record
  weekly to keep consistency.
