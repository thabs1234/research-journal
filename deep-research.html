1 · arXiv Agentic-AI Digest (automated)

source: researcher profile cron A daily, token-free cron job queries arXiv for abs:"agentic" OR "large language model" OR "LLM agent", newest-first, and writes a dated markdown digest. Below is the 2026-08-25 run (8 papers). Full history lives in profiles/researcher/memories/research_digest/ and is mirrored in /source.

How to Train a Critic Stably and Efficiently — P. Qi, X. Zhou, W. S. Lee · 2608.23566
GRPO avoids a critic by sampling multiple responses; a reliable critic could estimate token-level advantage from one. Standard critic recipes are unstable — this studies that instability.
SWE Refactor Bench: long-horizon whole-repository stack migration? — D. Hong, Y. Chi, W. Li · 2608.23564
Can coding agents autonomously perform decade-scale migrations existing benchmarks can't test? New long-horizon eval.
Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness — S. Karten, A. L. Zhang, K. Thomas · 2608.23552
Open-source harness with a persistent IPython REPL following the Recursive Language Model abstraction for long-horizon coding workflows.
When "Do Not" Is Not Deny: CLAUDE.md vs built-in controls — T. Yan · 2608.23550
Measures the gap between natural-language "do not" instructions and Claude Code's hard deny in 481 public CLAUDE.md files. Directly relevant to agent safety.
The Interaction Tax: diversity loss in multi-agent teams — S. E. Ann, H. Liu, C. Tan · 2608.23541
Does multi-agent LLM interaction help or hurt? Finds communication can erase diversity under equal budgets.
EarthVerse: scientific agents across dynamic Earth systems — Z. Cui, X. Yin, Y. Tang · 2608.23525
Benchmark evaluating scientific agents on changing physical processes + natural hazards.
Action-Aligned Retrieval w/ pairwise multimodal reranking (person anomaly search) — T.-K. Nguyen et al. · 2608.23503
An Interactive Agent for Requirement-Driven Candidate Sourcing — Y. He, F. Li, X. Ru · 2608.23501
Framing "find me people from a description" as a requirements-engineering task for LLM agents.

2 · iJEP Studio — creator intelligence

source: ijep_studio_research/profile.md iJEP Studio is a Boston-based PhD structural-biology animation studio (TikTok @ijep.studio), 31.4K followers / 429.4K total likes / 98 videos, 0 following (pure broadcaster). Niche: cinematic 3D molecular-biology explainers, no face.

What wins

  • Signature pillar — Molecular Machines: ATP synthase, bacterial flagellar motor, ribosome, CRISPR-Cas9, DNA helicase, photosystem II.
  • Hook levers: superlative ("Most Powerful Molecular Motor Ever Discovered"), mystery, scale awe.
  • Format: cinematic 3D, no talking head, visual-first; B2B conversion via ijepstudio.com → paid animation gigs (labs, journals, pharma).
  • Takeaway: it's a studio brand, not a persona. Clone model: "stunning 3D science, no face, strong title, consistent pillar rotation."

Caveat: per-video like counts not retrievable — TikTok WAF blocked this host; no live logged-in tab. Likes/follower ratio (~13.7) is a reach signal, not full engagement rate.

Transferable lesson: audiences engage with the hidden mechanism of a real condition ("what really happens when…?", "a look inside") — not the outline of something familiar. Keystone hook: name the thing → show the steps/mechanism → flag the danger → soft CTA. This format is portable across languages and health systems — which is exactly what the SA stream exploits (next page).
